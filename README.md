# ScreenTake

ScreenTake is a native screen recorder and editor for Mac. Record your screen, then shape the video with cursor effects, smart zoom, backgrounds, a webcam overlay, and voiceover narration.

**Latest beta: 0.1.10 (build 12)** · Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) · macOS 13 Ventura or later

[Visit the website](https://sso-ss.github.io/ScreenTake/) · [Download the DMG](https://github.com/sso-ss/ScreenTake/releases/download/v0.1.10/ScreenTake-0.1.10-build12-local.dmg) · [Release notes](https://github.com/sso-ss/ScreenTake/releases/tag/v0.1.10)

## What you can do

- Record at 30 or 60 FPS with microphone and system audio.
- Add a webcam overlay and choose a background or canvas format.
- Adjust the cursor, highlight clicks, and add zoom effects.
- Record narration while watching your finished screen recording. Move and trim each narration take on its own audio strip, with separate waveform, volume, and mute controls.
- Trim and arrange clips, then save a MOV file.

## Install

1. Download the DMG on an Apple Silicon Mac.
2. Open it and drag **ScreenTake.app** into **Applications**.
3. Open ScreenTake and allow Screen Recording, Microphone, and Camera access when needed.
4. Make a short test recording and check the exported video and audio before sharing.

This beta is locally signed but **not notarized by Apple**. macOS may block the first launch. If you trust the download, try opening the app once, then look for **Open Anyway** under **System Settings → Privacy & Security**. Do not disable macOS security protections.

Smart zoom and recording reliability are still being refined. Cursor and zoom re-editing need capture data retained in the current app session; reopening a finished video does not restore it.

ScreenTake keeps your recordings on your Mac. Please remove private information from any screenshots or recordings you share in an [issue](https://github.com/sso-ss/ScreenTake/issues).

This repository contains the public website and beta installer.
