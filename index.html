What do I need to get started?+

An Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) running macOS 13 or later. Download the DMG, drag ScreenTake into Applications, and open it. Allow Screen Recording, and Microphone or Camera access if you use them. On newer macOS versions, screen permission may be called Screen & System Audio Recording.

Can I edit a video I already have?+

Yes. Import a video to edit its timeline, add manual zoom regions, and adjust the background or framing. Original cursor editing needs capture data from a ScreenTake recording or saved project; it cannot be recovered from a finished video.

Do my recordings stay on my Mac?+

Yes. Recordings and editable source files stay on your Mac. Save a .screentake project to continue editing later, or choose where to export your finished MOV file.

What should I know about the beta?+

ScreenTake is signed with a Developer ID certificate and the installer is notarized by Apple. Smart zoom, device reliability, and performance are still being refined. Make a short test recording and review your export before sharing. Read the release notes.

How do I update ScreenTake?+

Version 0.1.16 and later support ScreenTake → Check for Updates… in the app. Earlier builds need one manual installation from the current DMG. Save your project before updating.
