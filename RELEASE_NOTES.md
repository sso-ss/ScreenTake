# ScreenTake 0.1.10 · build 12

ScreenTake now lets you record narration while watching your screen recording. Each take has its own audio strip and waveform, and you can move, trim, mute, and adjust its volume independently. Original recording audio remains on a separate strip. Playback controls stay centered beneath the video.

This Apple Silicon beta requires macOS 13 or later. The DMG is locally signed but not notarized. Save your work, quit the old app, and drag ScreenTake into Applications. Make a short test recording and check the export before sharing.
