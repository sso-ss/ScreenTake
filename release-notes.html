ScreenTake 0.1.10

Record your screen, then add your voice.

What’s new

  • Record narration while watching your screen recording.
  • Move and trim each narration take on its own audio strip.
  • See original audio and voiceover waveforms, with separate volume and mute controls.
  • Playback controls stay centered beneath the video, with editing tools on the left and time and zoom on the right.
Download 0.1.10 for Apple Silicon

Install

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac and macOS 13 or later. Save your work and quit ScreenTake. Open the DMG, drag ScreenTake into Applications, then reopen it.

Test build

This build is signed and notarized by Apple. Make a short test recording and check your export before sharing. Smart zoom and recording reliability are still being refined.

Cursor and zoom re-editing rely on capture data retained in the current app session. Imported or reopened finished videos do not restore that data.
