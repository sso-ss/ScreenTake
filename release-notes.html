ScreenTake 0.1.12

More control over your camera, clearer exports, and the microphone you choose.

What’s new

  • Choose Overlay or Full Screen for each camera section, with zoom and framing controls for full-screen footage.
  • Optionally follow one face. The camera crops gently to keep the face in view and keeps your framing when no face is detected.
  • Use the main Split button for camera sections. Splitting a screen recording also splits its camera presentation.
  • Add smooth transitions between camera sections, with adjustable duration and motion. Turn them off for a direct cut.
  • Preview your camera directly in the recording canvas. Enabling the camera turns on the microphone; you can turn it off or use the microphone on its own.
  • Select a microphone above Record Voiceover, sharing the saved selection with the recording controls.
  • Preserve source resolution through screen exports and capture higher-quality camera footage for full-screen layouts.
Download 0.1.12 for Apple Silicon

Install

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac and macOS 13 or later. Save your work and quit ScreenTake. Open the DMG, drag ScreenTake into Applications, then reopen it. Update reminders open a download page; installation remains manual.

Beta notes

The app and installer are signed with a Developer ID certificate, and Apple has notarized the installer. Make a short test recording and check your export before sharing. Smart zoom and device reliability are still being refined.

Face tracking follows one face. Long clips may take a moment to prepare, and tracking depends on a visible face. Cursor and zoom re-editing rely on capture data retained in the current app session; imported or reopened finished videos do not restore that data.
