ScreenTake 0.1.20

Add styled text, shape your camera with background removal, refine audio, and use the updated AI connection.

What’s new

  • Add styled text overlays and work with separate video, camera, audio, zoom, and text timeline tracks.
  • Choose Background Removed for a clean camera cutout in the live preview and exported video.
  • Adjust recorded audio independently, control gap closing after cuts, and remove pauses from added audio tracks.
  • Use the updated built-in MCP helper to control these editor features from a compatible AI client. Restart its MCP connection after installing the update.

Included from 0.1.19

  • Upload, replace, or remove a custom background image. Saved projects embed the image, and previews, recordings, and exports share the same background.
  • Choose 16 wallpapers in a four-column grid of square thumbnails. New 4K Silk, Pastel, Breeze, and Mint presets share translucent folds in four palettes.
  • Draw, move, and resize manual zoom regions, including on imported videos and phone layouts.
  • Adjust phone corner rounding separately and use floating phone chrome.

Verified custom background import, orientation, resizing, persistence, project portability, undo/redo, preview, and video export.

Included from 0.1.18

  • Rebuild edited audio without encoding the finished video a second time when audio levels are unchanged. This avoids the stalled processing path and preserves rendered video quality.
  • Show the processing stage after audio merging completes, so later work no longer stays labeled “Merging audio”.
  • Delete the selected timeline strip immediately, use smoother drag previews, and choose zoom transition motion that is retained in saved projects.

Verified the final audio-processing step against the affected recording, decoded the recovered video and audio through the end, and checked linked-audio timing and volume adjustments.

Included from 0.1.17

  • Reduce temporary disk use during imported-video exports while preserving original media, current results, Undo references, and saved projects.
  • Keep generated frames evenly spaced during long exports with many zooms, including imported videos with sparse frame timing.

Included from 0.1.16

  • Check, download, and install updates inside ScreenTake with Sparkle. Background checks use a persistent menu bar badge.
  • Enable automatic downloads and installation on quit in Settings → Updates. Automatic installation is optional and off by default.
  • Recording, media processing, and unsaved projects block a restart. Save your editable project first; deferred restarts can be resumed with Install and Relaunch in Updates settings.
  • Update feeds and downloads are signed and verified before installation.

Install 0.1.16 or later from the DMG once to enable the new updater. Builds through 0.1.15 require a manual installation.

Included from 0.1.15

  • Export more reliably when the video encoder is busy. Failed or cancelled exports preserve an existing destination file, and failures are logged for troubleshooting.
  • Edit with shortcuts for playback, frame stepping, splitting, undo/redo, audio linking, timeline zoom, applying changes, and export. Open the timeline’s keyboard button for the full shortcut reference.
  • Choose Mirror Camera before recording or while editing. Mirroring applies consistently to the live camera preview, editor preview, and exported video, and is retained in saved projects. Existing projects keep their original orientation.

Included from 0.1.14

  • Open the editor sooner after stopping. Effects render when you apply changes or download your finished video.
  • Keep native screen detail, including odd-sized window captures, with original-size content placed at 1×.
  • Keep the timeline ruler, playhead, and timestamp synchronized during playback and seeking.
  • Save editable projects, edit recorded audio clips separately, and crop browser content.
  • Choose an app language and optionally connect AI clients to the local editor.
  • Apply optional face-tracked camera beauty and makeup filters, with faster live previews.
  • Find ScreenTake in the menu bar and see an update badge until the available release is installed.
  • Keep the three-panel purple app icon introduced in 0.1.13.

Included from 0.1.12

  • Choose Overlay or Full Screen for each camera section, with zoom and framing controls for full-screen footage.
  • Optionally follow one face. The camera crops gently to keep the face in view and keeps your framing when no face is detected.
  • Use the main Split button for camera sections. Splitting a screen recording also splits its camera presentation.
  • Add smooth transitions between camera sections, with adjustable duration and motion. Turn them off for a direct cut.
  • Preview your camera directly in the recording canvas. Enabling the camera turns on the microphone; you can turn it off or use the microphone on its own.
  • Select a microphone above Record Voiceover, sharing the saved selection with the recording controls.
  • Preserve source resolution through screen exports and capture higher-quality camera footage for full-screen layouts.

Install

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac and macOS 13 or later. Save your work and quit ScreenTake. Open the DMG, drag ScreenTake into Applications, then reopen it. This one-time manual installation enables in-app updates. Use ScreenTake → Check for Updates… or enable automatic downloads and installation on quit in Settings → Updates.

Beta notes

The app and installer are signed with a Developer ID certificate, and Apple has notarized the installer. Make a short test recording and check your export before sharing. Smart zoom and device reliability are still being refined.

Face tracking follows one face. Long clips may take a moment to prepare, and tracking depends on a visible face. Saved .screentake projects retain source media and capture data for later editing. Imported finished videos support manual zoom regions, but do not include original cursor capture data.
