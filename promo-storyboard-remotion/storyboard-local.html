ScreenTake promo: a video that edits itself

A 48-second film for ScreenTake, the Mac screen recorder and editor. It borrows techniques from four reference promos (Raycast, Linear, Screen Studio and Rive). The film shows ScreenTake's features by using them on itself: the camera zooms on clicks, the cursor changes style, and near the end the film's own frame changes shape.

≈ 48 s 16:9 master · 1920 × 1080 + 9:16 cut, ≈ 20 s UI redrawn in code Music + UI sound, 150 BPM Copy from the ScreenTake website

Look

A dark, cinematic product film built from ScreenTake's own world: its dark UI, its purple and its wallpapers.

Ground

Near-black, lit by the Midnight, Lagoon, Prism and Ember glows with fine grain. The app UI sits in its real dark greys, #1c1c1e and #2c2c2e.

Hero

The product itself: the window, the cursor, the zoom. The presenter only appears inside the webcam bubble.

Colour

One accent, ScreenTake purple #6C5CE7. The four wallpapers bring all the other colour.

Type

SF Pro, as on the ScreenTake site: large, bold, tightly tracked headlines. The Rive-style cards use small uppercase labels in mono.

Camera

Always moving, with an extreme close-up open, depth of field, a tilted perspective, and motion blur on fast pull-outs and zooms.

Motion

Fast and sharp: quick expo snaps, hard cuts on the 150 BPM beat, and overshoot only on the cursor and bubble.

Depth (z-space)

Every shot is built as layers at real depths inside one 3D space, not as flat layers. When the camera moves, near layers move faster than far ones (parallax), focus blur depends on distance from the focus plane, and shadows grow with the gap between layers. The camera travels through the space (dolly), not by scaling the picture.

Captions and HUD labelsz +300
Webcam bubblez +240
Cursor and click ringz +180
Overlay panels, lifted clipsz +100
App window and timeline (focus plane)z 0
Blurred copies for depth of fieldz −300
Wallpaper glow and grainz −900
  1. 01A real dolly back from the Record button: the toolbar edges slide past the lens faster than the wallpaper behind.
  2. 02Title letters fly in from z +600 and sharpen as they reach the focus plane. The window drops back to z −400 and blurs.
  3. 03The overlay panel slides in at z +100, so it casts a real shadow across the toolbar.
  4. 04Smart zoom is a camera dolly toward the click point. The cursor sits above the page and shifts against it (parallax) while the camera follows.
  5. 05The camera flies through the title cards, set as planes one behind another, so each hard cut lands on the next plane.
  6. 06The bubble lifts toward the camera on each corner hop, its shadow spreads, and it settles back.
  7. 07On the tilted timeline, a dragged clip lifts to z +100 and drops into the closed gap. The detected pauses rise off the track before removal.
  8. 08Each new wallpaper arrives from far behind. On each ratio change the canvas turns slightly on its vertical axis and back.
  9. 09The swapping words come forward from depth. The wordmark focus pull is a real change of focus distance.

Limits: turns stay small (≤ 18°, except the timeline tilt), captions are always sharp while they're on screen, and the camera never moves so fast that UI text becomes unreadable.

Accuracy rules

The film only shows what the current beta does.

Redraw only real UI

The UI is rebuilt in code from the real screenshots and the app's design tokens. Use only real controls, labels and values, with no invented buttons.

The presenter is a still photo

No lip-sync and no voiceover, so the bubble shouldn't appear to speak. All movement comes from the bubble, not the face.

Pauses are reviewed

Always show “2 pauses” → Remove. Never show pauses being cut automatically.

Smart zoom is experimental

Say so in the small print on the end card.

No re-editing imported videos

Zoom and cursor re-editing need the original recording from the same session.

No hidden or unreleased features

Leave out the Duo layouts (hidden in the app) and the iPhone editor (a local prototype).

The iPhone frame is framing only

The app can't capture directly from an iPhone.

Beta status

Don't imply the app is notarized, on the App Store or runs on Intel Macs.

No version number

The download page says 0.1.9 but the website says 0.1.8, so leave it off the end card.

Its own look

Screen Studio is in the same category, so use ScreenTake's purple, dark UI and wallpapers, not Screen Studio's backdrop.

Sound

The music is locked to 150 BPM, so one beat is 0.4 s and a bar is 1.6 s. That matches the Raycast word swap and Linear's 0.4 s edge rhythm, so cuts, swaps and draws all land on the beat.

Decisions

All four are settled. The plan is ready for production.

Decided

UI redrawn in code

A real recording can't be animated: the zoom, cursor morphs, tilted timeline and reframing all need live layers. Every screen is rebuilt from ScreenTake's screenshots and design tokens (#6C5CE7, #1c1c1e, #2c2c2e, the track colours).

Decided

Webcam: the supplied presenter photo

A 1024 × 1024 still, cropped tight on the face for the circle and rounded-square bubble. The tight crop keeps the wall poster and mug text out of frame, so mirroring won't show backwards lettering.

Decided

Sound on

Music plus UI sounds, following the sound map above.
